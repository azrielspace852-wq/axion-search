Privacy Policy Last updated: Oct 2026

AXION Search is built around one principle: your search history is none of our business.

1. What we do NOT collect

  • No personal information, no accounts, no sign-up.
  • No tracking cookies, no advertising identifiers, no fingerprinting.
  • Search queries are not logged persistently. They are used only to fetch results in-memory and are discarded after the request completes.
  • We do not build a profile about you. We do not sell data. There is no data to sell.

2. Where your data lives

Settings (theme, language, AI on/off, AI model, SafeSearch) and your search history are stored only in your browser — in localStorage and ordinary first-party cookies (synced for server-side language/safe preferences). You can export, import, or wipe them at any time from the Settings page. Clearing your browser data removes everything.

3. Search providers (disclosed here only)

To deliver rich results, AXION Search aggregates and rotates across multiple public search sources. Your query is forwarded to these providers only at fetch time, with a generic browser User-Agent — never with your identity. Providers may include: Google, Bing, DuckDuckGo, Mojeek, YouTube, Google News, and the iTunes Search API. The provider used for any given query is never shown in the UI and rotates automatically for reliability. The AI summary is generated by Google Gemini / Gemma models via Google AI Studio, using only the snippets returned by the search above as context (grounded — no hallucination).

4. Cookies

AXION uses only functional, first-party cookies (max-age 1 year) to remember your language, theme, and SafeSearch preference — exactly like mainstream search engines. No third-party cookies, no analytics cookies.

5. AI summary

When enabled, your query and the top search snippets are sent to the selected AI model to produce a grounded summary. The model is instructed to answer only from the provided context and to explicitly state when information is not available. You can turn AI off entirely in Settings.

6. Hosting

This instance runs on GitHub Actions behind a Cloudflare Tunnel (domain axion-search.axn.cc.cd). Cloudflare may see encrypted TLS traffic termination as part of normal tunnel operation. No request content is stored by us.

7. Your rights

Because we store nothing about you, there is nothing to request, correct, or delete. To remove all local data, use Settings → Clear history / export, or clear your browser site data.

Questions? Contact the AXION team through your usual AXION channel. This policy is intentionally short — if it is not written here, we do not do it.
