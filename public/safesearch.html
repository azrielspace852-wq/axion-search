SafeSearch

SafeSearch filters explicit adult content from all result types (web, images, videos, news, audio). It is on by default and can be toggled by anyone using this device.

How it works

  • Query-time negative terms are appended to filter explicit results upstream.
  • Returned results are re-filtered against a blocklist of known explicit domains and keywords.
  • YouTube requests use YouTube's restricted-mode parameter when SafeSearch is on.
  • AI summaries are generated only from filtered results.

Turn SafeSearch on or off

Go to Settings → SafeSearch, or use the toggle below. Your preference is saved locally (cookie + localStorage) and applies to every search on this device.

Important
